<div id="about"></div>

<p class="lead" style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.75; color: var(--ac-text-main);">
I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Huaiyin Normal University (HYTC), which I joined in September 2026. Prior to this, I received my Ph.D. (2026) and M.S. (2022) in Computer Science and Technology from the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT) under the supervision of Prof. Jie Liu, Prof. Zeyao Mo, and Prof. Xinhai Chen, and my B.S. from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU) in 2020.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 0.98rem; line-height: 1.7; color: var(--ac-text-muted);">
My primary research interest lies in AI for Science (AI4S), with a particular focus on AI for Computational Fluid Dynamics (AI for CFD). Specifically, my work centers on intelligence-driven mesh generation, adaptive movement, and quality optimization, exploring how Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Reinforcement Learning (RL), and unsupervised learning techniques can overcome fundamental bottlenecks in complex geometric physical simulations.
</p>

    I am actively seeking self-motivated graduate and undergraduate students who are enthusiastic about AI4S, AI for CFD, and geometric deep learning; a solid background in mathematics and programming (e.g., PyTorch / C++) is preferred. Beyond academic research, I am keen to bridge the gap between AI algorithms and real-world engineering challenges. I warmly welcome collaborations with academic peers, industry partners, CAE software developers, and research institutes on topics such as intelligent mesh generation, simulation acceleration, and customized solver workflows. Feel free to reach out via email to discuss potential research collaborations or joint R&D projects.
