<p class="lead" style="font-size: 1.08rem; line-height: 1.75; color: var(--ac-text-main);">
Welcome to my personal academic homepage! I am an <b>Assistant Professor</b> at the <b>School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Huaiyin Normal University (HYTC)</b>, where I joined in September 2026. Prior to this, I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from the <b>National University of Defense Technology (NUDT)</b> in 2026, advised by Prof. Jie Liu. I earned my M.S. degree from NUDT in 2022 and B.S. degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU) in 2020.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 0.98rem; line-height: 1.7; color: var(--ac-text-muted);">
My research lies at the intersection of <b>Deep Learning</b>, <b>AI for Science (AI4S)</b>, and <b>Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)</b>, with a core focus on <b>Intelligence-driven Mesh Generation, Movement, and Quality Optimization</b>. I explore how Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Reinforcement Learning (RL), and unsupervised learning techniques can overcome fundamental bottlenecks in geometric physical simulations.
</p>
